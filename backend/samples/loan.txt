PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 12 October 2026 between QuickCash Finance Pvt. Ltd. ("the Lender") and Ms. Meena Iyer, Chennai ("the Borrower"), accepted through the QuickCash mobile app.

1. LOAN AMOUNT. The Lender agrees to lend Rs. 1,50,000 to the Borrower.

2. TENURE AND EMI. The loan is repayable in 24 monthly instalments of Rs. 9,650 each, due on the 3rd of every month.

3. INTEREST. Interest is charged at 2.5% per month on a flat basis. The interest rate is floating and linked to the Lender's internal benchmark.

4. FEES. A processing fee of 4% of the loan amount will be deducted at disbursal, along with other charges as applicable from time to time.

5. RATE CHANGES. The Lender may revise the interest rate at its sole discretion without prior notice.

6. PREPAYMENT. The Borrower may close the loan early by paying a foreclosure charge of 5% of the outstanding principal.

7. LATE PAYMENT. Delayed EMIs attract penal interest of 3% per month, compounded monthly and added to the outstanding loan.

8. RECOVERY. In case of default, the Lender and its recovery agents may contact the Borrower, her family members and the references provided, at any time, by phone, message or visit.

9. APP PERMISSIONS. By accepting this agreement, the Borrower allows the QuickCash app to access her phone contacts, photo gallery and location for verification and recovery.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This agreement is governed by the laws of India, and courts in Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Accepted electronically by the Borrower on 12 October 2026.
