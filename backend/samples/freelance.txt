FREELANCE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Zephyr Retail Pvt. Ltd., Bengaluru ("the Client") and Ms. Sana Qureshi, independent designer, Hyderabad ("the Designer").

1. SCOPE. The Designer will create a brand identity for the Client: logo, colour palette, typography and a 20-page brand guideline.

2. FEES. The total fee for the project is Rs. 2,40,000.

3. PAYMENT. The fee will be paid within 90 days after the Client's final approval of all deliverables, at the Client's discretion.

4. REVISIONS. The Designer shall make unlimited revisions until the Client is fully satisfied.

5. OWNERSHIP. All designs, drafts and ideas become the property of the Client as soon as they are created, whether or not payment has been made.

6. TERMINATION. The Client may terminate this agreement at any time, without paying for work in progress.

7. LIABILITY. The Designer shall indemnify the Client against all losses, claims and damages of any kind, without limit.

8. EXCLUSIVITY. During this agreement and for 12 months after it ends, the Designer shall not work for any other retail brand.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY. Both parties shall keep each other's confidential information private.

10. TIMELINE. The first concepts will be delivered within 3 weeks of signing.
