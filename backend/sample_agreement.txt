RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 25 October 2026 at Bengaluru between Mr. Suresh Rao ("Landlord") and Ms. Priya Sharma ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. The Landlord lets the flat No. 304, Lakeview Residency, HSR Layout, Bengaluru to the Tenant for residential use for a period of 11 months starting 1 November 2026.

2. RENT. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 (Rupees Eighteen Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of every month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant shall pay an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 90,000 (Rupees Ninety Thousand only) before taking possession.

4. REFUND OF DEPOSIT. The deposit shall be refunded at the sole discretion of the Landlord after deducting painting, cleaning and any other charges the Landlord considers necessary.

5. RENT INCREASE. The Landlord may increase the rent by up to 10% at any time during the tenancy.

6. LOCK-IN. There is a lock-in period of 6 months. If the Tenant leaves before the lock-in ends, one month's rent shall be forfeited from the deposit.

7. REPAIRS. All repairs to the premises, including structural repairs, plumbing and electrical faults, shall be carried out at the Tenant's cost.

8. INSPECTION. The Landlord or his agent may enter and inspect the premises at any time without prior notice.

9. TERMINATION BY LANDLORD. The Landlord may terminate this agreement for any reason by giving 15 days' notice, and the Tenant shall vacate the premises accordingly.

10. UTILITIES. Electricity and water charges shall be paid by the Tenant as per meter readings. The Landlord may disconnect electricity and water supply if any payment is delayed.

11. NOTICE BY TENANT. The Tenant may vacate the premises by giving 2 months' written notice to the Landlord after the lock-in period.
